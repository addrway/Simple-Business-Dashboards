# SBD — Simple Business Dashboard: Logistics Intelligence Platform Vision

## MISSION
Transform SBD into the easiest logistics intelligence platform on the market.
Users should feel: "I uploaded my file and instantly understood my logistics business."

This should feel like:
* Power BI made simple
* Tableau for logistics companies
* AI logistics analyst built in
* Beautiful SaaS experience
* Executive command center for operations

DO NOT BUILD JUST A DASHBOARD. BUILD A LOGISTICS INTELLIGENCE PLATFORM.

## CORE VISION
SBD is focused exclusively on logistics and operations.
Target customers:
* Trucking companies
* Freight brokers
* Distribution companies
* Warehouses
* Delivery companies
* Supply chain operators
* Fleet managers
* Operations managers
* Logistics consultants

Future industries can be added later. For Version 1: Focus only on logistics.

## PRIMARY MODULES

### 1. Executive Command Center
**Purpose:** Allow owners and executives to understand business performance within 30 seconds.
**KPIs:** Revenue, Loads, Gross Margin, Cost Per Load, Profit Per Load, Fleet Utilization, Driver Utilization, On-Time Delivery %, Delayed Shipments, Damage Claims.
**AI Actions:** Analyze My Business, Executive Summary, Find Profit Leaks, Suggest Improvements.

### 2. Fleet Operations
**KPIs:** Total Trucks, Active Trucks, Idle Trucks, Maintenance Vehicles, Fleet Utilization %, Fuel Cost Per Mile, Miles Driven, Maintenance Cost.
**AI Insights:** Underutilized assets, Maintenance risks, Fuel inefficiencies.

### 3. Shipments & Delivery
**KPIs:** Total Shipments, Delivered, In Transit, Delayed, Damaged, Exception Rate.
**Charts:** Shipment Trends, Route Performance, Delay Analysis, Delivery Performance.
**AI Insights:** Delay causes, Route bottlenecks, Customer service risks.

### 4. Warehouse Operations
**KPIs:** Capacity, Utilization, Throughput, Pick Accuracy, Inventory Accuracy.
**Charts:** Throughput Trend, Capacity Usage, Productivity Metrics.
**AI Insights:** Bottlenecks, Space optimization, Labor recommendations.

### 5. Financial Performance
**KPIs:** Revenue, Fuel Cost, Labor Cost, Maintenance Cost, Insurance Cost, Gross Profit, Operating Margin.
**Charts:** Revenue Trends, Expense Trends, Cost Breakdown, Margin Analysis.
**AI Insights:** Cost reduction opportunities, Profitability improvements, Expense anomalies.

**Capabilities:**
* Analyze My Data

* Find Profit Leaks
* Predict Risks
* Suggest KPIs
* Generate Executive Summary

* Suggest Cost Savings / Operational Improvements

## CSV MAGIC EXPERIENCE
This is the core differentiator.
User uploads CSV/JSON. SBD automatically:
* Detects columns, metrics, categories
* Generates KPIs, charts, AI insights, executive summaries
The user should feel: "I uploaded a spreadsheet and instantly understood my business."

## REPORTING ENGINE
Generate: PDF Reports, CSV Exports, Executive Summaries, Weekly/Monthly/Quarterly Reports, AI Insights.

## ADMIN SYSTEM
Use `profiles.is_admin`.
Admin users receive: Unlimited AI, Dashboards, Templates, Reports, Exports, Future Features.
Admin users never see upgrade prompts.

## SUBSCRIPTION SYSTEM
**User Types:** Trial, Pro, Admin. No permanent free plan.
* **Trial:** 24 hours, 5 AI analyses, 10 AI chats.
* **Pro:** $29.99/month (Unlimited everything). Stripe integration required.

## DESIGN SYSTEM
**Inspired by:** Stripe, Linear, Ramp, Retool, Vercel.
**Visual Style:** Premium, Modern, Luxurious, Logistics-focused, Executive-grade, Data-driven.
**Use:** Existing SBD styling, Tailwind, Playfair Display headings, Figtree body font, Soft shadows, Responsive layouts.

## DEPLOYMENT
Support: Supabase, DigitalOcean, Nginx, PM2, React Frontend, Node/Express Backend. Maintain Vercel compatibility during transition.

## SUCCESS METRIC
A logistics company owner should be able to upload a file and receive Executive, Fleet, Shipment, Warehouse, and Financial Dashboards, plus AI Insights and Business Recommendations within minutes.
